About Us

KNOW US

Founded in 2000, Oracle Group is a recognized manufacturer and exporter of plastic food packaging products. A network of around 300 channel partners supports customers across all the major cities of the country.

Our products are well established with domestic and overseas customers alike. Built on a strong reputation and many years of cooperation, Oracle Group is a trusted supplier of plastic food packaging products to buyers in the USA, UK, Canada, Australia, Fiji, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, UAE, Kuwait, Singapore, Cyprus, New Zealand, Iraq, Colombia, Mauritius, Aruba, Thailand and Madagascar.

We are deeply committed to meet the needs of our customers. “Total Customer Satisfaction” is Our Ultimate Goal.
