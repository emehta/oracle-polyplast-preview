About Us

KNOW US

We, M/s. Oracle Group founded in the year 2000 are a recognizedmanufacturer & exporter of plastic food packaging products. We have a very strong business network supported by around 300 channel partners across all the major cities of the country.

Our products have been well accepted by domestic as well asoverseas customers. Based on the good reputation & many years ofco-operation the group has been chosen as a trusted supplier ofplastic food packaging products by the overseas customers from USA, UK, Canada, Australia, Fiji, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, UAE, Kuwait,Singapore, Cyprus, New Zealand, Iraq, Colombia, Mauritius, Aruba,Thailand, Madagascar.

We are deeply committed to meet the needs of our customers. “Total Customer Satisfaction” is Our Ultimate Goal.
