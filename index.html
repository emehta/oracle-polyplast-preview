KNOW US

We, M/s. Oracle Group founded in the year 2000 are a recognized manufacturer & exporter of plastic food packaging products. We have a very strong business network supported by around 300 channel partners across all the major cities of the country.

Our products have been well accepted by domestic as well as overseas customers. Based on the good reputation & many years of co-operation the group has been chosen as a trusted supplier of plastic food packaging products by the overseas customers from USA, UK, Canada, Australia, Fiji, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, UAE, Kuwait, Singapore, Cyprus, New Zealand, Iraq, Colombia, Mauritius, Aruba, Thailand, Madagascar.
